Lesson 5: NLP (Natural Language Processing)</h2>

                <h3>Definition</h3>

                <p>
                    <strong>Natural Language Processing (NLP)</strong> is a branch of
                    <strong>Artificial Intelligence (AI)</strong> that enables computers
                    to understand, interpret, process, and generate human language, such
                    as text and speech.
                </p>

                <h3>Real Examples</h3>

                <ul>
                    <li>💬 ChatGPT</li>
                    <li>🌐 Google Translate</li>
                    <li>✍️ Grammarly</li>
                    <li>🗣️ Alexa</li>
                    <li>📱 Siri</li>
                </ul>

                <h3>NLP Tasks</h3>

                <ul>
                    <li>🌍 Translation</li>
                    <li>📂 Text Classification</li>
                    <li>😊 Sentiment Analysis</li>
                    <li>📝 Summarization</li>
                    <li>❓ Question Answering</li>
                    <li>🤖 Chatbots</li>
                </ul>

                <h3>Workflow</h3>

                <div class="diagram-box">
                    Text
                    <br>↓
                    <br>Tokenization
                    <br>↓
                    <br>Embedding
                    <br>↓
                    <br>Model
                    <br>↓
                    <br>Answer
                </div>

                <div class="highlight-box">
                    <h3>📌 Interview Tip</h3>

                    <p><strong>Q: What is the difference between NLP and LLM?</strong></p>

                    <p><strong>Answer:</strong></p>

                    <p>
                        <strong>NLP (Natural Language Processing)</strong> is the field of
                        AI that focuses on understanding and processing human language.
                    </p>

                    <p>
                        <strong>LLMs (Large Language Models)</strong> are advanced AI
                        models built using NLP techniques (especially Transformers) that
                        can generate, summarize, translate, answer questions, and perform
                        many language-related tasks.
                    </p>

                    <p>
                        <strong>In simple words:</strong>
                    </p>

                    <ul>
                        <li><strong>NLP</strong> = The field of study</li>
                        <li><strong>LLM</strong> = A powerful application of NLP</li>
                    </ul>
                </div>
