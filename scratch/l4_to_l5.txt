                <h3>Definition</h3>

                <p>
                    A <strong>Neural Network (Artificial Neural Network - ANN)</strong>
                    is a computer model inspired by the human brain that learns patterns
                    from data using interconnected artificial neurons to make predictions
                    and decisions.
                </p>

                <p>
                    It consists of connected artificial neurons.
                </p>

                <h3>Structure</h3>

                <div class="diagram-box">
                    Input Layer
                    <br>↓
                    <br>Hidden Layer(s)
                    <br>↓
                    <br>Output Layer
                </div>

                <h3>Structure of a Neural Network</h3>

                <p>A neural network usually has three types of layers:</p>

                <div class="diagram-box">
                    Input Layer
                    <br>↓
                    <br>Hidden Layer(s)
                    <br>↓
                    <br>Output Layer
                </div>

                <h4>1. Input Layer</h4>

                <p>
                    Receives the input data.
                    <br><strong>Example:</strong> Image pixels, text, numbers.
                </p>

                <h4>2. Hidden Layer(s)</h4>

                <p>
                    Processes the information.<br>
                    Learns patterns and relationships in the data.<br>
                    A network can have one or many hidden layers.
                </p>

                <h4>3. Output Layer</h4>

                <p>
                    Produces the final prediction or result.
                </p>

                <h2>📖 Types of Neural Networks</h2>

                <h3>1. Feed Forward Neural Network (FNN)</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    A <strong>Feed Forward Neural Network (FNN)</strong> is the simplest
                    type of neural network in which data flows in one direction—from the
                    input layer, through the hidden layer(s), to the output layer—without
                    any feedback loops.
                </p>

                <h3>2. Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    A <strong>Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)</strong> is a type of
                    deep neural network specifically designed to recognize and analyze
                    images by automatically detecting features such as edges, shapes, and
                    objects.
                </p>

                <h3>3. Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    A <strong>Recurrent Neural Network (RNN)</strong> is a type of neural
                    network designed to process sequential data by remembering information
                    from previous inputs, making it suitable for tasks involving time or
                    ordered sequences.
                </p>

                <h3>4. Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    <strong>Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)</strong> is an advanced type of
                    Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) that can remember important information
                    for long periods while forgetting unnecessary information, making it
                    effective for long sequences of data.
                </p>

                <h3>5. Transformer Networks</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    <strong>Transformer Networks</strong> are advanced deep learning
                    models that process entire sequences in parallel using a
                    self-attention mechanism, enabling them to understand relationships
                    between words or data efficiently. They are the foundation of modern
                    <strong>Large Language Models (LLMs)</strong> such as ChatGPT, Claude,
                    and Gemini.
                </p>

                <h2>📖 Applications of Neural Networks</h2>

                <h3>1. Image Recognition</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    Image Recognition is the application of neural networks to identify,
                    classify, and recognize objects, people, or scenes in digital images.
                </p>

                <h3>2. Voice Recognition</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    Voice Recognition is the application of neural networks that enables
                    computers to recognize, understand, and convert human speech into text
                    or commands.
                </p>

                <h3>3. AI Chatbots</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    AI Chatbots are intelligent software applications powered by neural
                    networks and Large Language Models (LLMs) that can understand,
                    process, and generate human-like conversations.
                </p>

                <h3>4. Medical Diagnosis</h3>

                <h4>Definition</h4>

                <p>
                    Medical Diagnosis is the application of neural networks to analyze
                    medical data, images, and patient records to help detect diseases,
                    predict health conditions, and support doctors in making accurate
                    diagnoses.
                </p>
