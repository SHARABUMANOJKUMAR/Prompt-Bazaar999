Module 4: Advanced Prompting Techniques

📖 Lesson 1: Chain of Thought (CoT)

Definition

Chain of Thought (CoT) is an advanced prompting technique that encourages the AI to solve a problem by breaking it into logical, step-by-step reasoning before giving the final answer.

Instead of directly predicting the answer, the AI reasons through the problem in multiple steps.

Why is it Important?

Chain of Thought helps AI:

  • Solve complex problems.
  • Improve reasoning accuracy.
  • Reduce mistakes.
  • Explain its thinking process.
  • Handle mathematics and logical reasoning better.

Characteristics

  • Step-by-step reasoning
  • Logical thinking
  • Better accuracy
  • Easy to understand
  • Suitable for complex tasks

Uses

Mathematics, Programming, Logical Reasoning, Data Analysis, Scientific Problems

Workflow

Problem
↓
Reason Step 1
↓
Reason Step 2
↓
Reason Step 3
↓
Final Answer

Real-Time Example

Prompt:
Solve this math problem step by step:

If a train travels 60 km in one hour, how far will it travel in 5 hours?

Real-Life Analogy

Imagine solving a math problem in school. Your teacher gives marks not only for the final answer but also for showing every calculation step. Chain of Thought works the same way.

📌 Interview Tip

Q: What is Chain of Thought Prompting?
Answer: Chain of Thought Prompting encourages the AI to solve problems using logical step-by-step reasoning before producing the final answer.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Use Chain of Thought for: Coding, Mathematics, Business Analysis, Scientific Reasoning.

📖 Lesson 2: Self Consistency

Definition

Self Consistency is a prompting technique where the AI generates multiple reasoning paths for the same problem and selects the most consistent or frequently occurring answer.

Instead of relying on a single reasoning process, the AI compares several possible solutions before producing the final response.

Why is it Important?

It improves:

  • Accuracy
  • Reliability
  • Logical consistency
  • Confidence in the final answer

Characteristics

  • Multiple reasoning paths
  • Majority voting
  • Better accuracy
  • Reduced reasoning errors

Uses

Mathematics, Logical puzzles, Scientific calculations, AI decision-making

Workflow

Question
↓
Reasoning Path 1
↓
Reasoning Path 2
↓
Reasoning Path 3
↓
Most Consistent Answer

Real-Time Example

Ask AI to solve the same math problem three different ways and choose the answer that appears most often.

Real-Life Analogy

Three experienced doctors independently diagnose the same patient. If all three reach the same conclusion, confidence in the diagnosis increases.

📌 Interview Tip

Self Consistency improves reasoning by comparing multiple reasoning paths instead of relying on just one.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Use Self Consistency for important reasoning tasks where accuracy matters more than speed.

📖 Lesson 3: Tree of Thought (ToT)

Definition

Tree of Thought (ToT) is an advanced prompting technique where the AI explores multiple possible reasoning paths, evaluates each one, and selects the best solution before generating the final answer.

Unlike Chain of Thought, which follows a single reasoning path, Tree of Thought explores several branches before deciding.

Why is it Important?

Tree of Thought helps AI:

  • Explore multiple solutions.
  • Compare alternatives.
  • Improve decision-making.
  • Solve complex planning tasks.

Characteristics

  • Multiple branches
  • Decision making
  • Evaluation of alternatives
  • Better planning
  • Flexible reasoning

Uses

Business Strategy, Game Playing, Planning, Research, Optimization Problems

Workflow

Problem
↓
Idea A → Idea B → Idea C
↓
Evaluate Ideas
↓
Best Solution

Real-Time Example

Prompt:
Suggest three marketing strategies for launching a new AI product. Compare them and recommend the best one.

Real-Life Analogy

Imagine standing at a road intersection. You evaluate three different routes before choosing the fastest one.

📌 Interview Tip

Tree of Thought explores multiple reasoning branches, while Chain of Thought follows a single reasoning path.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Use Tree of Thought when the problem has multiple possible solutions.

📖 Lesson 4: Prompt Chaining

Definition

Prompt Chaining is a technique where multiple prompts are connected together, and the output of one prompt becomes the input for the next prompt.

This allows complex tasks to be completed in smaller, manageable steps.

Why is it Important?

  • Breaks large tasks into smaller tasks.
  • Improves accuracy.
  • Makes workflows easier to automate.
  • Reduces prompt complexity.

Characteristics

  • Sequential
  • Modular
  • Reusable
  • Easy to debug

Uses

AI Workflows, RAG Systems, AI Agents, Document Processing

Workflow

Prompt 1
↓
Output
↓
Prompt 2
↓
Output
↓
Prompt 3
↓
Final Result

Real-Time Example

1. Summarize an article.
2. Extract key points.
3. Generate presentation slides.

Each step uses the previous output.

Real-Life Analogy

Cooking a meal: Buy ingredients → Prepare vegetables → Cook → Serve.
Each step depends on the previous one.

📌 Interview Tip

Prompt Chaining connects multiple prompts into one workflow.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Use Prompt Chaining for long workflows instead of writing one huge prompt.

📖 Lesson 5: Reflection Prompting

Definition

Reflection Prompting is a technique where the AI reviews its own answer, reflects on it, and suggests improvements before providing the final response.

Why is it Important?

  • Improve quality.
  • Reduce mistakes.
  • Make responses clearer.
  • Increase completeness.

Characteristics

  • Self-review
  • Continuous improvement
  • Higher quality
  • Better explanations

Uses

Essay Writing, Coding, Reports, Documentation

Workflow

Question
↓
Initial Answer
↓
Reflection
↓
Improved Answer

Real-Time Example

Prompt:
Write an essay, then review it and improve clarity, grammar, and completeness.

Real-Life Analogy

A student writes an assignment, rereads it, corrects mistakes, and submits the improved version.

📌 Interview Tip

Reflection Prompting asks AI to review and improve its own work.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Always add a reflection step before generating important business or technical content.

📖 Lesson 6: Self Critique

Definition

Self Critique is a prompting technique where the AI evaluates its own response, identifies weaknesses or mistakes, and suggests corrections.

Why is it Important?

  • Accuracy
  • Quality
  • Reliability
  • Logical consistency

Characteristics

  • Self-evaluation
  • Error detection
  • Continuous improvement
  • Better reasoning

Uses

Coding, Research, Technical Writing, Business Reports

Workflow

Answer
↓
Find Errors
↓
Suggest Improvements
↓
Revised Answer

Real-Time Example

Prompt:
Write Python code.

Now critique your code and improve it.

Real-Life Analogy

A software developer reviews their own code before submitting it for code review.

📌 Interview Tip

Self Critique focuses on identifying flaws, while Reflection Prompting focuses on improving the overall response.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Use Self Critique for high-quality reports, research, and code generation.

📖 Lesson 7: Verification Prompting

Definition

Verification Prompting is a prompting technique where the AI checks whether its answer is correct, complete, and supported by evidence before presenting the final response.

Why is it Important?

  • Reduce hallucinations.
  • Improve trust.
  • Increase factual accuracy.
  • Detect inconsistencies.

Characteristics

  • Fact checking
  • Validation
  • Evidence-based
  • Reliable

Uses

Healthcare, Law, Finance, Scientific Research

Workflow

Question
↓
Generate Answer
↓
Verify Facts
↓
Final Verified Response

Real-Time Example

Prompt:
Answer the question and verify whether each fact is supported by reliable sources.

Real-Life Analogy

A journalist verifies information from multiple sources before publishing a news article.

📌 Interview Tip

Verification Prompting checks whether the answer is correct before delivering it.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Use Verification Prompting whenever factual accuracy is critical.

📖 Lesson 8: Iterative Prompting

Definition

Iterative Prompting is a technique where prompts are repeatedly refined based on previous responses until the desired result is achieved.

Why is it Important?

  • Continuous improvement.
  • Better quality.
  • More precise outputs.
  • User feedback integration.

Characteristics

  • Repeated refinement
  • Feedback-driven
  • Flexible
  • High-quality results

Uses

Logo Design, Content Writing, Software Development, AI Image Generation

Workflow

Initial Prompt
↓
AI Response
↓
User Feedback
↓
Improved Prompt
↓
Better Response

Real-Time Example

1. Generate a website homepage.
2. Improve colors.
3. Improve typography.
4. Improve animations.
5. Final professional UI.

Real-Life Analogy

An architect creates multiple building drafts, improving each version based on client feedback until the final design is approved.

📌 Interview Tip

Iterative Prompting is an ongoing refinement process that gradually improves the output through multiple prompt-and-feedback cycles.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Start with a simple prompt, review the output, provide specific feedback, and repeat until the result meets your requirements.
