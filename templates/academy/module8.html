📘 Module 8: AI Image Prompt Engineering

🎯 Objective

Learn how to write professional prompts for AI image generation platforms and create high-quality logos, product images, marketing posters, and other visual content.

📖 Lesson 1: Midjourney

Definition

Midjourney is an AI-powered image generation platform that creates high-quality artistic and realistic images from natural language prompts.
It is widely used for concept art, branding, illustrations, and creative design.

Why is it Important?

  • Midjourney produces:
  • Professional artwork
  • High-quality concept designs
  • Creative illustrations
  • Cinematic visuals

Characteristics

  • Artistic style
  • High-quality rendering
  • Excellent lighting
  • Strong composition
  • Rich textures

Uses

  • Logo Concepts
  • Character Design
  • Game Art
  • Architecture
  • Branding

Workflow

Prompt
↓
Midjourney AI
↓
Image Generation
↓
High-Quality Artwork

Real-Time Example

Create a futuristic AI company logo featuring a glowing blue fox, minimal style, flat vector, white background, modern branding, professional, 1:1 aspect ratio.

Real-Life Analogy

Imagine giving instructions to a professional digital artist who transforms your ideas into artwork.

📌 Interview Tip

Midjourney is best known for creating artistic, cinematic, and visually stunning AI-generated images.
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Always include:
  • Subject
  • Style
  • Lighting
  • Camera
  • Colors
  • Aspect Ratio
  • Quality

📖 Lesson 2: DALL·E

Definition

DALL·E is an AI image generation model developed by OpenAI that creates and edits images from text descriptions.
It is especially useful for illustrations, product concepts, editing existing images, and creative visual design.

Why is it Important?

  • DALL·E supports:
  • Image generation
  • Image editing
  • Object replacement
  • Background modification
  • Creative illustrations

Characteristics

  • Accurate prompt understanding
  • Strong editing capabilities
  • Easy to use
  • Creative image generation

Uses

  • Marketing Graphics
  • Product Design
  • Illustrations
  • Posters
  • Social Media

Example

Design a modern coffee shop poster using warm colors, realistic style, professional lighting, and minimalist typography.
📌 Interview Tip

DALL·E is particularly strong at following detailed instructions and editing images based on text prompts.

📖 Lesson 3: FLUX

Definition

FLUX is a modern text-to-image AI model designed to generate highly realistic, detailed, and visually accurate images from natural language prompts.

Why is it Important?

  • FLUX is known for:
  • Photorealistic quality
  • Strong prompt understanding
  • Excellent text rendering
  • Detailed compositions

Characteristics

  • Realistic
  • Sharp details
  • Natural lighting
  • High accuracy

Uses

  • Product Photography
  • Fashion
  • Portraits
  • Advertising
  • Architecture

Example

Generate a luxury smartwatch placed on a black marble table with cinematic lighting, realistic reflections, premium product photography, 8K quality.

📖 Lesson 4: Stable Diffusion

Definition

Stable Diffusion is an open-source AI image generation model that allows users to create, customize, and fine-tune images from text prompts.

Why is it Important?

  • Stable Diffusion provides:
  • Full customization
  • Local deployment
  • Fine-tuning
  • Community extensions
  • Flexible workflows

Characteristics

  • Open source
  • Custom models
  • Flexible
  • Extendable

Uses

  • AI Art
  • Character Design
  • Product Images
  • Research
  • Creative Projects

Example

Generate a cyberpunk city at sunset with neon lights, rainy streets, ultra-detailed, cinematic atmosphere.

📖 Lesson 5: Ideogram

Definition

Ideogram is an AI image generation platform known for producing high-quality images with accurate text rendering, making it especially useful for logos, posters, typography, and branding.

Why is it Important?

  • Ideogram excels at:
  • Typography
  • Posters
  • Logo Design
  • Social Media Graphics
  • Branding

Characteristics

  • Excellent text rendering
  • Clean layouts
  • Modern branding
  • High-quality typography

Uses

  • Logos
  • Posters
  • Business Cards
  • Flyers
  • Marketing

Example

Create a modern technology logo for "AI LeXa Lawyer" using blue and purple gradients, minimalist design, transparent background.

📖 Lesson 6: Composition

Definition

Composition is the arrangement of visual elements within an image to create balance, focus, and visual appeal.

Why is it Important?

  • Good composition:
  • Improves readability
  • Guides attention
  • Creates professional visuals
  • Enhances storytelling

Characteristics

  • Balance
  • Symmetry
  • Rule of Thirds
  • Visual Hierarchy
  • Depth

Uses

  • Photography
  • Posters
  • Logos
  • UI Design

Example Prompt

Create a centered composition with the subject occupying the middle third of the image and a clean background.

📖 Lesson 7: Camera Angles

Definition

Camera angles define the viewpoint from which an AI-generated image is created, influencing perspective, emotion, and storytelling.

Common Camera Angles

  • Eye Level
  • High Angle
  • Low Angle
  • Bird's Eye View
  • Worm's Eye View
  • Close-up
  • Wide Shot
  • Macro

Example

Generate a close-up product photograph of a luxury perfume bottle using a macro camera angle.

📖 Lesson 8: Lighting

Definition

Lighting controls how light interacts with the subject, influencing mood, realism, depth, and atmosphere.

Common Lighting Types

  • Natural Light
  • Soft Light
  • Hard Light
  • Golden Hour
  • Blue Hour
  • Studio Lighting
  • Rim Lighting
  • Cinematic Lighting

Example

Portrait with soft natural lighting and warm golden-hour sunlight.

📖 Lesson 9: Color Theory

Definition

Color Theory is the study of how colors work together to create visual harmony, contrast, emotion, and branding.

Common Color Schemes

  • Monochromatic
  • Complementary
  • Analogous
  • Triadic
  • Warm Colors
  • Cool Colors

Uses

  • Branding
  • Marketing
  • UI Design
  • Product Photography

Example

Use a complementary blue and orange color palette with premium luxury branding.

📖 Lesson 10: Aspect Ratio

Definition

Aspect Ratio defines the proportional relationship between the width and height of an image.

Common Ratios

RatioUse
1:1Instagram, Logos
16:9YouTube, Presentations
9:16Reels, Shorts
4:5Instagram Portrait
3:2Photography

Example

Create a modern product advertisement with a 16:9 aspect ratio.

📖 Lesson 11: Negative Prompts

Definition

Negative Prompts tell the AI what should not appear in the generated image.

Why is it Important?

  • Negative prompts help:
  • Remove unwanted objects
  • Improve image quality
  • Reduce visual artifacts
  • Produce cleaner results

Example

Negative Prompt:
blur,
low quality,
watermark,
extra fingers,
text,
logo,
distorted face
💡 Prompt Engineer Tip

Always use negative prompts for realistic images.

📖 Lesson 12: Character Consistency

Definition

Character Consistency ensures the same character maintains a consistent appearance, clothing, facial features, hairstyle, and proportions across multiple generated images.

Why is it Important?

  • It helps create:
  • Comics
  • Storybooks
  • Animations
  • Brand Mascots
  • Game Characters

Characteristics

  • Same face
  • Same clothing
  • Same hairstyle
  • Same accessories
  • Same proportions

Example Prompt

Generate the same female AI teacher wearing a blue blazer, black hair, glasses, smiling, consistent facial features across all images.
